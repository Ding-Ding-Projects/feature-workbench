## Fresh-eye review
1. Poster showed the opening, all bars. The loop now opens on the payoff and `poster` equals that first frame. Seed cards carry real titles: "Try the command palette" (To do), "Write the next useful step" (In progress); compact uses "Pick a language".
2. Loop seam at about 23.9s (card vanished, hole, count mid-roll). There is no seam reset now: the card leaves through its own Done press, sliding and fading while its room closes, and the loop ends on the same frame it begins.
3. Pop-ins. The Fly and its handoff are gone: one card element travels in by transform, fading in over 0.14s where it first overlaps the dimming To do copy. The To do card is unhidden only as its slot opens and hidden again only after it has closed. Its leave fades before it is hidden. Still listed by film: "appears with no entrance" on a lane Count. Every count change in the loop now rolls; I could not find the frame it points to, and fixing it would need a kit change. "appears, then moves" on the travelling card: it takes over from an identical card at the same spot, and a solid card should not fade across its trip.
4. Blurred, ghosted labels during the move. The card's buttons no longer swap sets: the first button's label is a `Roll variant="fade"` changing in place, Done stays put, and the save step's States crossfade has its blur and rise removed.
5. Card overflowing its body. The browser is now taller than its content (600 wide, 560 compact), so nothing runs past the body. The In progress room is a fixed 96px height for the 88px card plus its gap.
6. Empty band when a card leaves. On Done the card fades and slides while the room closes from 140ms later, so the room is never seen empty. On the move to In progress the To do copy dims while the travelling card covers it.
Remaining candidate lines: "near aligned" between the title and a button label. The button's edge is on the title's edge and the label sits in its padding, as in the product. "settle snap" on a title at 390 was not traced further.
Not checked: dark theme, touch, real-device frame pacing (capture coverage reports sampling gaps), host page embedding.

## v2 redesign (Material 3 Expressive)
What changed: look.css now carries the v2 roles; every hard-coded v1 colour in board.css was replaced. Lanes are tonal containers with 32px corners (To do #DEE1F9 / ink #161B2C, In progress #FFDCBE / #2D1600, Done #DDE1FF / #001257) and the counts sit in white pill badges. Cards are 20px corners with no hairline. The search field and framed icon buttons became the Expressive floating toolbar (a #E9E7EF pill holding a white filter field, History and Undo). New task became the extended FAB (primary container, 16px corners, hover grows to 22px, press tightens to 12px). On wide cards the two move buttons became the v2 connected button group "To do | In progress | Done": the selected segment is a filled full pill with a check that opens its own room, neighbours keep 4px inner corners. Compact cards keep one tonal pill move. The saved notice is the inverse-surface (#303036) full-pill snackbar. Stage is 32px corners (28 compact), lanes widened to 244 (compact 154) so the group fits, browser moved to left 104.
Motion: the card's travel, the slot opening and closing, the leave, the title field and the snackbar rise all use `--cp-spring-default`; segment and button shape morphs use `--cp-spring-fast`; colour and opacity use `--cp-effects`.
Beats (about 15s, opening on the payoff): hold … 1 Done segment pressed, the selection morphs onto Done, the card and its room leave together (In progress 2 to 1) … 2 New task, an empty card drops into To do … 3 "Export a backup" typed and saved (To do 2 to 3) … 4 In progress segment pressed, the selection morphs, then the card travels on the spring, counts roll … payoff: snackbar grows in and holds; last frame equals the first.
