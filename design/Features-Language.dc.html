Action sound effects

Plays a short, quiet sound when an action really finishes, and goes silent the moment you switch it off.

What it must do

  • Play a short cue only when an action really finishes, never for a failed or cancelled one.
  • One Settings switch turns every cue off at once; the change is immediate and survives a restart.
  • Respect existing mute, volume and quiet-hours preferences; playback never blocks the action.
  • Every cue has a visible and screen-reader equivalent, so nothing is lost with sound off or unavailable.

Lets you turn on only the focus and attention aids that help you, each one on its own.

What it must do

  • Five separate switches: Focus, Low stimulation, Time awareness, One thing at a time and Momentum. No master switch.

Sets how playful messages sound in English and in Cantonese separately, from fully serious to most playful, without changing any fact.

Food discovery at startup

Now and then at startup, briefly shows one dim sum dish with its English and Cantonese names and a photo, then gets out of the way.

What it must do

  • Draw once per launch with a 10% chance; if it hits, show one randomly chosen dish, never more than once.
  • Show the English and Cantonese names and the photo, with alt text naming the dish. It dismisses itself, never delays startup and never takes focus.
  • Never show during first-run setup, an error, an update or a task in progress. Respect reduced motion and quiet settings.
  • There is no off switch: any old one is removed and stored preferences carry forward. The shared distraction-reduction mode hides it completely.
  • Photos come only from the public photo catalog described in the next card.

verifiedDraw rate and one show per launch, absence in every excluded flow, no third-party CDN or tracking, hidden in distraction-reduction mode.

Verified food photograph source

Shows dish photos only from the one public photo catalog, and says plainly when a photo is missing instead of filling the gap.

What it must do

  • The only source is the public dim sum photo catalog: its catalog/index.json names are authoritative and its published catalog-v1 release assets are the only photos.
  • Load photos by their public asset URL or from an application-data cache that records the source URL and revision.
  • Never generate, download, scrape or copy photos into this repository; any old copies are moved out.
  • A missing photo is left out with a visible unavailable state and reported, never filled in locally.
  • The archive repository is for recovery and audit only, never a live source.
