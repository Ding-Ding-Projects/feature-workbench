Accessible controls and text sizing

Lets you set text size, contrast, motion and target size from a menu that is always within reach.

What it must do

  • Keep an accessibility menu visible and reachable on every page: text size, contrast, reduced motion, visible focus, larger targets and keyboard help.
  • Hiding the menu asks first, with Cancel focused, and keeps every preference; settings search and reset bring it back.
  • No text or control is clipped, overlapped or pushed off-screen at 100, 125, 150 or 200% scale, in either language.
  • Read-aloud is started by the person and never speaks secrets.

verifiedProof needed: captures at every scale and narrow width, plus keyboard-only and screen-reader passes.

Full color picker and format conversion

Lets you pick any color precisely and copy it in the format you need.

What it must do

  • Every color control is a continuous field and hue slider with typed values, never swatches alone.
  • Converts between named colors, HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV, HWB, Lab, LCH, OKLab, OKLCH and CMYK, keeping alpha.
  • Names the active color space, warns before a value falls outside the gamut, and shows contrast.
  • An animated rainbow option settles on one hue when reduced motion is on.

verifiedProof needed: round-trip conversions in both directions with no silent loss.

Gives every screen one consistent Material Design 3 look, in light and dark, with readable contrast.

What it must do

  • Every page follows Material Design 3 Expressive: color, type, shape, elevation, motion and component anatomy.
  • Every control is a registered Material Design 3 component; plain HTML is only document structure.

Keeps menus and panels readable, on screen, and resizable, remembering where you put them.

What it must do

  • Menus, popovers, tooltips and panels paint their own background, border and shadow.

Live charts in the Liveline style

Shows numbers that change while you watch as one calm, smoothly moving line, so the latest value and its direction are clear at a glance.

What it must do

  • One smooth line eases toward each new value, led by a pulsing live dot with an up, down or flat arrow; the axis range, grid labels and value badge move together.
  • A soft fill below the line comes from the series colour, the grid stays quiet, and time labels follow your language and clock setting.
  • Scrub with the pointer or the arrow keys to see a crosshair with time and value; a screen-reader summary gives the latest value and trend.
  • Real loading, paused, empty and stale states; the chart never invents points to look alive.
  • Under reduced motion or Low stimulation the line snaps instead of easing and the dot stops pulsing, with every value still readable.

verifiedProof needed: a timed recording of a live update in both motion states, pointer and keyboard scrubbing, every honest state, and no point the source did not send.
