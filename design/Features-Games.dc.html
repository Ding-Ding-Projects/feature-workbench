Shared Roblox model integration

Makes sure every shared model is actually placed and used in the game, with evidence for each.

What it must do

  • Use every model in the shared catalogue, nested models included, in a real place in the game.
  • Restore each original and verify it against its recorded hash before use.
  • Keep a hand-written ledger: each model's destination, what the player does with it, and the evidence.
  • An unused copy, hidden placeholder or storage-only entry never counts; pending models stay listed as pending.

Native Roblox visual realism

Holds every object in a Roblox game to a believable, real-world look and working behaviour.

What it must do

  • Buildings, furniture, animals, characters and machinery look like the real thing.
  • A plain block, or a block with a painted-on label, never stands in for the object.
  • Working machines show believable form, materials and support, visibly operate, and show a timer on an in-world gauge.
  • Accept only after checking in-game screenshots, ground contact, connections and working interactions.
